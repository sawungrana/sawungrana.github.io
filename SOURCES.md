# SOURCES.md — licences and provenance

Every image and font on this site, with provenance. If something here is wrong,
it is a bug.

## Plates (maps)

All plates are public-domain or CC0 scans of Dutch East Indies maps, from museum
collections via Wikimedia Commons. They are cropped and converted to navy
duotone by `tools/plates.py`. Originals live in `assets-src/plates/`, which is
git-ignored; re-download from the links below.

The paper texture is an inline SVG noise filter, generated in CSS, not an image
file.

### Why the duotone curve is not the same as lantip.space

The sibling site uses engravings, which are mostly paper with dark lines. Maps
are the opposite: large mid-tone fields where the sea and the land shading sit.
At the engraving settings the whole plate reads as a grey slab, so `plates.py`
raises the black point, drops gamma below 1 and blends toward paper per plate.
The `fade` value is the knob; lower means quieter.

## Ornaments (guilloche)

`header-band.svg` and `rosette.svg` are generated by `tools/guilloche.mjs` in the
[lantip.space](https://lantip.space) repo, which is an original implementation of
standard spirograph mathematics. `divider.svg` is drawn by hand for this site,
because the lantip wave is 1200 units wide and renders sub-pixel at the rule
width used here.

## Portrait

`public/portrait.webp` and `portrait.jpg` are resized from `assets-src/photo.png`
by hand. Not a stock image.

## Generated files

`public/cv.pdf`, `public/cv-plain.pdf` and `public/og.png` are produced by
`scripts/render.mjs` from the site itself. They are committed so that a link to
the CV does not depend on a build succeeding.
